About Us

Our Story Begins with Compassion

Founded in 2022 and officially registered in 2024, Contra-Congenital Foundation is dedicated to ensuring every child with congenital anomalies receives the care and support needed for a full, healthy life through research, healthcare, and awareness initiatives.

Core Values That Guide Us

  • Compassion – Putting children and families first
  • Excellence – Delivering high-quality care
  • Innovation – Pioneering new solutions
  • Collaboration – Working with partners
  • Integrity – Maintaining transparency

Our Mission

To improve the health and well-being of children with congenital anomalies through comprehensive education, compassionate support, and strong advocacy efforts that create systemic change.

Our Vision

We envision a world where every child, regardless of congenital challenges, enjoys equal opportunities for a healthy, happy childhood and a bright future filled with possibilities.

Our Programs

Comprehensive Support Initiatives

We have developed targeted programs to address all aspects of congenital conditions through research, care, and community engagement.

Community Programs

Support groups, public awareness campaigns, and educational workshops designed to empower families and build supportive communities.

Learn More

Advocacy Programs

Driving policy changes, building strategic partnerships, and fundraising to promote equity and support for affected individuals.

Learn More

Clinical Programs

Early intervention services, telemedicine solutions, and specialized clinics to improve healthcare access and outcomes.

Learn More

Research Programs

Pioneering studies on environmental factors, maternal health, and genetic influences to prevent congenital conditions.
