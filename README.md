# 🌍 Contragenital Awareness Initiative Website

This is the official website of the **Contragenital Awareness Initiative (CAI)**, a nonprofit organization dedicated to educating, supporting, and advocating for individuals affected by contragenital health conditions. The site is built using modern **HTML5**, **CSS3**, and **JavaScript**, and is designed to be responsive, accessible, and informative.

---

## 📖 About the NGO

The **Contragenital Awareness Initiative** is a grassroots NGO focused on addressing issues related to contragenital health conditions — conditions that arise congenitally or early in life and often go underreported or misunderstood.

### Our Mission:
- Raise awareness about contragenital conditions.
- Provide support resources for individuals and families.
- Advocate for inclusive healthcare systems.
- Partner with medical professionals and educators for outreach.

We believe in equal access to health education, diagnostics, and treatment regardless of location or socioeconomic status.
